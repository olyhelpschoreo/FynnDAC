What we're asking

We'd rather over-explain this than have you find out in week three. Here's the whole commitment.

The window
90 days, starting .
Scheduled time
About four hours total across three months.
  • Three monthly email check-ins, 15 minutes each
  • One 60-minute group session with the founders
  • Two 30-minute one-on-one sessions with our marketing lead
  • Ongoing Slack channel, as much or as little as you want
Daily use
You and your partner both set up accounts and actually use Fynn to run your family. This only works if it's your real calendar and your real week.
Feedback we'll ask for
What's working, what's broken, what's missing. Plus your read on pricing and on the way we're talking about Fynn publicly. And a short survey at the end on whether your household actually feels different.
Saying so publicly
If Fynn works for you, we'll ask you to say so (a short video, a written line we can quote, and a family photo for our marketing). We'll also ask you to mention it to people you think it would help: your league, your group chat, your neighbors.

That last one is the real ask. The year free isn't payment for feedback; it's an even trade. We get a product shaped by the exact families it's built for. You get the tool, first, free, and made partly by you.

FAQ

Does my partner have to participate?

Yes. Fynn is built for a household, not a person. You're our primary contact and main tester, but your partner needs an account and needs to use it. A lot of what we're testing is what happens between the two of you.

What happens after the free year?

You choose. Council members will be offered our founding rate, locked in, for as long as you stay. No auto-charge, no surprise. We'll tell you well before the year ends.

How much time is this really?

About four hours of scheduled calls and check-ins over 90 days. The daily use isn't extra time — it's replacing the tools and texts you already use to run your week.

Who sees my family's information?

Your family data is yours. We never sell it, and we never use your family's details in marketing without asking you first and showing you exactly what we want to use.

What if it's not working for me?

Tell us. That's more useful than a polite exit. If you need to step out of the program, you keep the free year and we part on good terms.

When do I hear back?

We review applications weekly and reply either way. If you're a fit, we'll set up a short call before you start.
